# Ripple Support

Ripple is a mobile app that lets citizen-science volunteers monitor lake water quality in the field. Using a handheld sensor and their phone, volunteers record GPS-tagged readings with photos in under three minutes, and conservation groups get automatic alerts when a reading suggests a real problem like contamination or an algae bloom.

## Installing Ripple

- **iPhone:** download Ripple from the App Store.
- **Android:** download the Ripple installer (APK) from our [release page](https://github.com/fgajarsa-sudo/Ripple/releases/tag/cac-2026-submission) and open it. Because Ripple isn't on the Google Play Store yet, Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, go back, and tap **Install**. This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.
