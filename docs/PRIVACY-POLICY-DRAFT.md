# Ripple Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

Ripple is operated by its student developers. This policy explains what information Ripple collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, a display name you choose, and your confirmation that you are 13 or older (required to use Ripple).

When you submit a water quality reading, we collect the GPS location where you took the reading, the date and time, weather conditions, the sensor values you enter, an optional photo of the water, and any notes you add.

If you enable notifications, we collect a device push token so your conservation group can send you alerts.

## Location data

Ripple only records your location at the moment you submit a reading, and only with your permission. Ripple does not track your location in the background, and does not request background location access on either iOS or Android.

## How your data is shared

Your readings, photos, and submission history belong to the conservation group you join. Admins and reviewers in your group can see your submissions, including your name and exact location, so they can respond to water quality concerns. Your data is never visible to members of a different conservation group.

Ripple's own team does not see your name, your email, your exact location, your photos, or any other information that could identify you. We only have access to anonymized, aggregated trends (for example, average readings for a lake over time), with all personal information removed.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## Third-party services we use

Ripple relies on a small number of service providers to operate:

- **Supabase** hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
- **Anthropic's Claude API** analyzes submitted photos and sensor readings to help flag readings that may need a closer look. Submitted photos and readings are sent to this service for that purpose.
- **Expo** delivers push notifications to your device.
- **Sentry** helps us catch and fix crashes and errors. Sentry does not receive your name, email, or other identifying information from Ripple.

Each of these providers only receives the data necessary to perform their specific function.

## Your choices and controls

From within the app, you can:

- **Export your data** — download a CSV of every reading you have personally submitted.
- **Delete your account** — permanently removes your profile and login. Your conservation group keeps the readings you submitted, but they are no longer linked to your name or account once your account is deleted.

## Data retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile information is removed, but your group's submission records are retained in anonymized form, since that data belongs to the conservation group that collected it.

## Children's privacy

Ripple is not intended for use by anyone under 13. We require an affirmative confirmation of age at sign-up and do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this same address.
